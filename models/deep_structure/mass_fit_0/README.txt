Here I fitted a model of Vindija, Yoruba, Loschbour and Stuttgart lineages 
based on a simplifed version of the continuous migration/merger model from 
Aaron's 2023 paper. This model was fitted to the segment of the curve r>=1e-5
for speed and because I was considering switching to this bin set at the time I
ran it. Several pulse time parameters were fixed (T_H_N, T_Los_Stu) and the 
number of parameters is quite large. Stem 2 contributes ancestry to Yoruba 
(30% in the best-fitting model) and also to Stuttgart, e.g. to the Basal 
Eurasian lineage (48% in the best-fitting model). I am not entirely happy with 
the topology of Yoruba formation/OOA separation.

Fits are relatively good, especially in the long-distance part of the curve. 
Several fits terminated prematurely due to bound issues following the initial 
round of optimization (I think the problem parameter was p_H_N, which went
everywhere to 0.20). This is an interesting behavior and must be due to the 
dilution of stem 1 ancestry by stem 2 in extant humans.
